The long-awaited NBA new season tips off on October 21st, which means only a dozen or so days remain. After an eventful offseason packed with storylines, multiple franchises have been reshaped in disruptive fashion.

## 1. The Blockbuster Trade: Jaylen Brown to Philly

Jaylen Brown was traded from Boston to Philly in exchange for Paul George and two first-round picks — a shocking deal for all NBA fans, especially supporters of the two long-time nemeses. No one expected Boston to part with a top-5 or top-10 league player and send him to their fiercest rival.

## 2. A Landmark Free Agency: LeBron James Signs with the Sixers

On the free-agent market, the league’s biggest signing has to be LeBron James joining the Sixers. This well-established veteran, entering his 24th season, signed a two-year veteran minimum contract with a player option in the second year. It was an entirely unexpected move that even Sixers fans did not see coming, sparking citywide revelry once his decision went public. 

This signing elevates the Sixers’ roster into one of the league’s most well-rounded groups. The starting five features:
- A former MVP
- A top-five player from last season
- A formidable opponent-respected star
- A talented young core piece
- The GOAT

This is a dream lineup for the Sixers. If everyone stays healthy, especially Joel Embiid, they will make a deep playoff run for sure.

## 3. Team Chemistry & Camaraderie

Building chemistry is always the top priority for a newly assembled roster. Judging from the two preseason games they have played, this team is no exception. The roster is undeniably talented, yet fully realizing its potential requires patience and time. 

These players are not only physically gifted but also possess high basketball IQs, which greatly facilitates chemistry building. Also, chemistry does not solely exist on the court; off-court chemistry inside the locker room and even during downtime plays a vital role in a successful team. You can sense strong chemistry from the bench vibe: every time a teammate scores, if everyone on the bench is cheering and hyped for him, this team has solid camaraderie.

## 4. The Biggest X-Factor: Joel Embiid's Health

The biggest uncertainty facing the Sixers’ outlook revolves around a familiar topic: health, specifically the health of Joel Embiid. Standing at 7 feet and weighing 122 kg, he moves as nimbly and skillfully as a point guard, but this comes with trade-offs. Every jump shot, crossover and rebound places stress on his knees. 

Plagued by injuries throughout his career, Embiid has appeared in only 90 games over the past three seasons. This fuels legitimate concerns about whether he can stay healthy through the season, especially since the Sixers lack a reliable backup center. Sources report the Sixers are considering aggressive load management for him, granting ample regular-season rest so he can stay in peak form for the playoffs.

## 5. Outlook: The Championship Window

One way or another, the next two seasons represent the best championship window the Sixers have had in decades. The players are motivated and fans are hyped. It is the perfect moment to hoist another championship banner at the Xfinity Mobile Arena for the City of Brotherly Love.
